## Rules
- The root loader strips a top-level `version` key from each owner file, requires it to equal 1 (ConfigError otherwise, naming the file), and passes the rest to the owner model unchanged. Owner models are not edited.
- `HernessConfig` fields: `app: AppSettings` (herness.reports.settings), `memory: MemoryConfig` (herness.harness.memory.settings; call `parse_injection_patterns` where T07-02's ledger says), `pipelines: PipelinesConfig` (already real — verify), `metrics`, `sources`, `resilience` as their owner modules define them. Remove `_AppStub`/`_MemoryStub`.
- Layering: config.py may import settings modules (settings exception); `_config_sections.py` is private to core and appears in the "core base is closed" and settings-leaves contracts exactly as config.py does.
- New YAML files carry `version: 1` and the spec defaults; nothing invented — where the spec gives no default, leave the key out and record it.
