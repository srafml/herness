# Writer: prior outcomes

This run has prior recommendations with outcomes. In addition to the report, write
`prior_outcomes_commentary` as one paragraph.

- For each prior recommendation in the input, state its outcome verdict (paid off, no effect,
  worse or inconclusive) as the `outcome` rows show it, and nothing the rows do not show.
- Write every number as a marker with a matching `NumberRef` from the outcome rows, and cite
  the verified findings the paragraph relies on in its `finding_ids`.
- When a prior recommendation has no outcome row, say "unknown" for it; never infer an outcome
  from other data.
- Keep the paragraph factual: no new recommendations and no blame.
